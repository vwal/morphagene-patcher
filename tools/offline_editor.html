How preset recall works

0% = fully counterclockwise, 50% = noon, 100% = fully clockwise. These are virtual control positions, following each control’s existing curve; they are not percentages of duration, speed or effect strength. Gene Size runs from longest on the left to shortest on the right. Varispeed uses its calibrated range.

Check a recall to apply it at startup and settings-only reload. After recall, soft pickup continues from the preset position: turn the matching control near or across that position to take over. Effects use their matching SHIFT + knob gesture. Knob and CV are summed, so CV can trigger pickup. Unchecked presets preserve their live values on reload; saves retain configured numbers rather than later knob movements.

Varispeed and return to 1:1

Classic (0) is the original bidirectional response with smooth braking around stop. V/oct (1) is the alternate bidirectional pitch response: with the input attenuverter carefully adjusted, CV can track volts per octave. Forward only (2) runs from stop at fully CCW to maximum forward speed. Extended range and Unity at noon reshape these responses; V/oct is not a promise of calibrated tracking in every modified layout. If an older build changed your layout to forward only, select your preferred layout here and save options.txt after upgrading.

Off keeps stock ranges. On gives +/-4x in vsop 0/1, or stop to forward 8x in vsop 2.

Optional and off by default. Hold SHIFT alone for at least 0.5 seconds, then release to recall exact forward +1x. Varispeed holds there until the combined knob/CV input picks it up. Short taps still advance splices. Moving a knob, pressing another button or card activity cancels the reset. Keep the SHIFT input unpatched.

Optional and off by default. Places forward +1x at calibrated noon in every layout, range and scale, with an easy-to-find 48-52% notch. Bidirectional layouts fit stop and reverse into the left half. With this off, no extra flat zone is added to continuous Varispeed.

0-100% of the calibrated control range, before layout, extended range and Unity at noon mapping. It is a knob position, not a playback-speed percentage. With vctr 1, 50% recalls forward +1x; otherwise speed depends on the selected layout and quantization. A later SHIFT unity recall replaces this hold.

Off: SHIFT + Varispeed locks to the scale. On: pitch is normally quantized; SHIFT + Varispeed temporarily becomes continuous. Release snaps back.

Applies to both quantization modes. Off disables quantization.

For octl 4: comma-separated 1-11, e.g. 3,5,7,10. Root and octaves are always included. 0 means root/octaves only.

Morph, loops and chord ratios

Always included in this firmware. On removes the silent gaps at minimum Morph. Higher overlap settings stay unchanged.

0-100% virtual knob position. For normal unclocked playback with independent density off, 10% selects the stock seamless-loop setting (9-11%). At 0%, Morph Loop Off leaves a scheduling gap; Morph Loop On removes it. Higher values add overlap, then pitch/pan variation. Clock and independent density can change Gene spacing.

Genes, Slide and density

Choose the original or smoother Gene envelope.

0-100% virtual knob position, not a percentage of the splice length. 0% and 1% play the longest Genes (the full splice); turning up shortens them, and 100% gives the shortest Genes. The response is nonlinear; actual duration also depends on the reel, speed and clock.

0-100% virtual knob position: move the Gene window from the beginning toward the end of the selected splice. Gene Size affects which portion is heard. This is not an absolute timestamp.

SHIFT + Slide position, 0-100%: 0% is no jitter, and increasing it widens Gene-start scatter. With sldm 1 this becomes repeating Turing spread. The response is nonlinear, not a percentage of reel length.

With dens 1, dstr selects startup timing. Its default is 1 Gene/sec. Hold SHIFT and turn Gene Size to select stock timing at fully CCW. Turn slightly clockwise for 0.1 Genes/sec, through 8 at noon to 128 at fully CW. Pitch and Morph can change while the selected rate stays fixed. Unshifted Gene Size sets length. Panel edits last until another adjustment, an explicit dstr reload or power-off. The module keeps the configured value in options.txt. Overrides the Turing rate gesture; external clock takes priority.

Check to recall the timing above on settings-only reload, including stock timing at 0. Uncheck to keep the live timing on reload and use 1.0 Gene/sec at startup. The module adds dstr back when it rewrites options.txt, so check the file again after a normal mount or options save.

Enter 0 for stock Gene timing or 0.1–128 for a rate in Genes/sec, in steps of 0.1. The default is 1.0 Gene/sec. With dens 1, SHIFT + Gene Size remains available after either choice. If your card already says dstr 8.0, it will still start at 8 Genes/sec. Change it to 0 for stock timing or 1 for one Gene per second. An external clock takes priority.

Recording, S.O.S. and feedback

Choose which button gesture starts a recording into the current splice and which creates a new splice.

For ordinary recording, S.O.S. blends the input with Gene playback. Choose input only to capture the live input at any S.O.S. position. Dry kill modes 2 and 3 use their own recording sources.

Mode 0 monitors the S.O.S. curve. Modes 1–3 monitor only Gene playback. Mode 1 keeps the ordinary recording source and SHIFT feedback controls. Mode 2 mixes with stored audio under the tape head on fixed overdubs. Mode 3 mixes with performed Gene playback, including Varispeed, Morph, Gene Size and Slide.

0 fades in existing audio up to noon, then fades out incoming audio. Both are at full level at noon. 1 keeps incoming audio full and fades in existing audio clockwise. 2 keeps existing audio full and fades out incoming audio clockwise. An omitted line selects 0 on startup or either card load. The first take on an empty reel always records full input. Choose sosc 1 with dryk 2 for the c4 full-input overdub.

Applies only to dryk 2. Fixed keeps the loop length and overdubs inside the Slide and Gene Size window. Extend works with sosc 0 or 1 and checks S.O.S. when REC starts. On the last or only splice, fully CCW (the lowest 4.5% of the effective knob/CV range, including a recalled S.O.S. preset) starts a replacement at the splice beginning. Input records forward at 1×, independent of Varispeed, Slide and Gene Size. Stop REC to set a longer end. A shorter take keeps the old length and tail. The selected recording mode lasts until REC stops. Earlier splices and takes started above the CCW zone use fixed-length overdubs with normal feedback.

Selects tape overdub or classic TLA feedback for SHIFT + S.O.S. and fbkp in dryk 0 and 1. Modes 2 and 3 use their dedicated recording paths and main S.O.S. knob.

SHIFT + S.O.S. virtual knob position for dryk 0 and 1. With tape 1, this sets how much of the old pass is retained; with tape 0 and inop 0, it sets the Gene playback contribution. Modes 2 and 3 take their feedback setting from the main S.O.S. knob or its preset (sosp).

Primary S.O.S. virtual knob position on the selected sosc curve. At 50% in the default crossfade, both incoming and existing audio are at full level.

Limits overlap while tape-dubbing. 0 disables the cap; 1-9 means 0.1-0.9. Higher values can increase glitches.

Playback effects

Selects one playback effect: sample-rate reduction or warble; they cannot run together. srrp and wrbp retain separate recalled amounts. Without an amount preset, startup uses the existing reset behavior. Warble uses less CPU than sample-rate reduction.

SHIFT + Morph position, 0-100%: 0% bypasses SRR; increasing it strengthens sample-rate reduction. Active with fxsl 1. This is the knob curve, not a bit-depth or sample-rate percentage. Stored separately from warble; fxsl 0 bypasses both effects.

SHIFT + Morph position, 0-100%: 0% is no warble; higher values add stronger wow/flutter. Active with fxsl 2. This is the knob curve, not a pitch percentage. Stored separately from SRR; only the selected effect runs.

Turing patterns

SHIFT + Organize: evolution; SHIFT + Slide: repeating scatter; SHIFT + Gene Size: Genes per step when dens is off.

SHIFT + Organize position, 0-100%, used with sldm 1. 0% locks the repeating pattern; 100% produces fresh randomness. Intermediate values follow the existing nonlinear knob curve, not a literal probability percentage.

Choose 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 or 16 Genes per step. Used with sldm 1; dens 1 takes priority over the Turing rate. An explicit zero recalls 1 Gene per step. Unchecked preserves the live Turing rate on reload.

Number of repeating random steps, 1–16. An explicit zero selects 1 step.

Chance of choosing the root rather than a chord ratio from mcr1-mcr3. 100 disables pitch changes.

Playback and splice selection

Classic checks the gate at the end of each Gene or splice. Momentary starts and stops with the gate. Trigger loop keeps playing and jumps to the start set by Slide and Organize on each rising edge.

Choose when a splice selected with Organize or SHIFT takes over.

Splice numbers run from 1 to 300. Both 0 and 1 recall the first splice. A number above the loaded reel count selects its last splice while keeping your configured number. Unchecked keeps normal startup selection and preserves the current splice on settings-only reload.

Input and diagnostics

Stock gain index, 0-3. REC held + SHIFT taps changes it on the module; the mod saves it after about 3 seconds.

Always copies L to both channels and ignores R. Auto copies L only while R is quiet.

Enable this to use the right signal input to modulate playback position while the left input has no signal.

Writes cpulog.txt periodically. Leave off for normal performance.
