The first time

Required

The first time, install your mod build using Make Noise’s official audio update procedure, just as you would an official firmware update.

The SD-card updater is itself one of the mods. It becomes available after this first audio install; subsequent updates can come from the card.

Follow the instructions supplied with Make Noise’s official update and Briskcat’s first-install guide.

Recovery: use the same audio procedure with the original official mg204.wav. The mods do not replace the bootloader.

  1. Build below and download the update WAV, mg204+c2.wav.
  2. Remove the SD card. Connect your computer or phone to Left Signal IN, at about 80% player volume. Disable effects, EQ, notifications and other sounds.
  3. Power on while holding REC. Only the SPLICE window lights green. Release REC.
  4. Play the update WAV from start to finish. The windows flash while it loads, then all lights flash when it is done.

    Do not touch the module or interrupt playback. If the update fails, power off and start again from step 3.

  5. Finish with Make Noise’s Varispeed calibration: power off, then power on holding SPLICE. Release SPLICE when its window lights green. Turn Varispeed fully counterclockwise and clockwise, press SPLICE, then power-cycle.
  6. After this first audio install, put mgmod.bin at the root of the card and power on for later updates. A successful update renames it mgmod.ok.

    THE EXTENDED VERSION

    From pitch to performance.

    A single build, with options for the way you play. All extended range, density, normal quantization and Morph Loop switches default to off.

    New in mg204+c2

    Return to 1:1

    Enable vuni 1, hold SHIFT alone for at least half a second, then release to return to exact forward +1x from any Varispeed position. Soft pickup holds the speed until the combined knob/CV input reaches or crosses it. Short SHIFT taps retain ordinary splice navigation.

    New in mg204+c2

    1:1 at noon

    Enable vctr 1 to place forward +1x at calibrated noon in full-range and bidirectional layouts, with every quantized scale. The 48-52% notch makes it easy to find in continuous mode too. Stop and reverse fit into the left half in bidirectional modes. Both unity options default to off.

    New in mg204+c2

    Recall the controls you choose

    All dialable values can now be included in prepared options: Morph, Gene Size, Slide, Varispeed, S.O.S. mix, feedback, effects, Turing evolution and Genes per step, alongside density and gain. Select a splice too. Each new recall is optional; unchecked values stay live during settings-only reload. Included controls resume from their recalled positions through soft pickup.

    New in mg204+c2

    Prepared effects and splices

    SRR and warble retain separate amounts; fxsl selects one or bypasses both. splc selects a numbered splice; a number above the reel count selects the last while retaining your configured number. Normal saves preserve configured presets, not subsequent knob movements.

    Pitch & timing

    Expanded Varispeed

    Enable vrng 1 for +/-4x, or combine it with vsop 2 for stop to forward 8x. Choose octaves, fifths, semitones or a custom scale. vqnt 1 keeps pitch quantized normally; SHIFT + Varispeed temporarily frees it. Default vqnt 0 retains SHIFT-to-lock.

    Pitch & timing

    Independent Gene density

    With dens 1, startup density comes from dstr, default 8 Genes/sec. SHIFT + Gene Size adjusts 0.1-128 Genes/sec, independently of pitch and Morph. Live knob changes are not saved across power cycles. Include dstr to recall a prepared rate on settings-only reload; omit it to keep the live rate. External clock takes priority; available voices limit dense, long Genes.

    Sound & movement

    Morph Loop and playback effects

    mlop 1 removes the gaps at minimum Morph; default mlop 0 retains them. The stock seamless-loop setting is also available with mpos 10 under normal unclocked scheduling. SHIFT + Morph controls SRR or warble, and SHIFT + Slide controls start jitter.

    Sound & movement

    Feedback and Turing patterns

    SHIFT + S.O.S. controls tape overdub or classic TLA feedback. With sldm 1, SHIFT + Organize controls evolution, SHIFT + Slide controls repeating spread, and SHIFT + Gene Size sets Genes per step when independent density is off.

    Live workflow

    Swap settings and keep playing

    Remove the idle card, hold SHIFT alone for one second, then insert a prepared matching card while still holding SHIFT. The live reel stays loaded and the card remains mounted for recording and saving. Explicit presets recall; omitted ones preserve their live state. An explicit splice recall intentionally changes selection.

    Everyday use

    Small conveniences

    Input gain is remembered. Mono input can copy L onto both channels. Updates can be installed from SD after the first audio install. Includes Briskcat’s splice-overdub fix and flash prefetch.

    OPTIONS.TXT

    Set up your starting point.

    Load the options.txt created by your Morphagene. Its first line, other stock settings and unknown lines are preserved. Recognized duplicates are reduced to one entry. Invalid ordinary values reset to their documented defaults. Optional recalls are unchecked when missing or invalid, and their numeric choices stay available for re-enabling. Only checked presets are included; module options saves preserve omitted preset keys. Review the generated file before saving.

    What do preset percentages mean?

    0% = fully counterclockwise, 50% = noon, 100% = fully clockwise. These are virtual control positions, following each control’s existing curve; they are not percentages of duration, speed or effect strength. Gene Size runs from longest on the left to shortest on the right. Varispeed uses its calibrated range.

    Check a recall to apply it at startup and settings-only reload. After recall, soft pickup continues from the preset position: turn the matching control near or across that position to take over. Effects use their matching SHIFT + knob gesture. Knob and CV are summed, so CV can trigger pickup. Unchecked presets preserve their live values on reload; saves retain configured numbers rather than later knob movements.

    Varispeed and return to 1:1

    Classic (0) is the original bidirectional response with smooth braking around stop. V/oct (1) is the alternate bidirectional pitch response: with the input attenuverter carefully adjusted, CV can track volts per octave. Forward only (2) runs from stop at fully CCW to maximum forward speed. Extended range and Unity at noon reshape these responses; V/oct is not a promise of calibrated tracking in every modified layout.

    Off keeps stock ranges. On gives +/-4x in vsop 0/1, or stop to forward 8x in vsop 2.

    Optional and off by default. Hold SHIFT alone for at least 0.5 seconds, then release to recall exact forward +1x. Varispeed holds there until the combined knob/CV input picks it up. Short taps still advance splices. Moving a knob, pressing another button or card activity cancels the reset. Keep the SHIFT input unpatched.

    Optional and off by default. Places forward +1x at calibrated noon in every layout, range and scale, with an easy-to-find 48-52% notch. Bidirectional layouts fit stop and reverse into the left half. With this off, no extra flat zone is added to continuous Varispeed.

    0-100% of the calibrated control range, before layout, extended range and Unity at noon mapping. It is a knob position, not a playback-speed percentage. With vctr 1, 50% recalls forward +1x; otherwise speed depends on the selected layout and quantization. A later SHIFT unity recall replaces this hold.

    Off: SHIFT + Varispeed locks to the scale. On: pitch is normally quantized; SHIFT + Varispeed temporarily becomes continuous. Release snaps back.

    Applies to both quantization modes. Off disables quantization.

    For octl 4: comma-separated 1-11, e.g. 3,5,7,10. Root and octaves are always included. 0 means root/octaves only.

    Morph and seamless looping

    Always included in this firmware. On removes the silent gaps at minimum Morph. Higher overlap settings stay unchanged.

    0-100% virtual knob position. For normal unclocked playback with independent density off, 10% selects the stock seamless-loop setting (9-11%). At 0%, Morph Loop Off leaves a scheduling gap; Morph Loop On removes it. Higher values add overlap, then pitch/pan variation. Clock and independent density can change Gene spacing.

    Genes, Slide and density

    Stock option: choose the original or smoother Gene envelope.

    0-100% virtual knob position, not a percentage of the splice length. 0% and 1% play the longest Genes (the full splice); turning up shortens them, and 100% gives the shortest Genes. The response is nonlinear; actual duration also depends on the reel, speed and clock.

    0-100% virtual knob position: move the Gene window from the beginning toward the end of the selected splice. Gene Size affects which portion is heard. This is not an absolute timestamp.

    SHIFT + Slide position, 0-100%: 0% is no jitter, and increasing it widens Gene-start scatter. With sldm 1 this becomes repeating Turing spread. The response is nonlinear, not a percentage of reel length.

    With dens 1, startup density comes from dstr (default 8 Genes/sec). Hold SHIFT and turn Gene Size to adjust it from 0.1 to 128 Genes/sec. Knob changes remain until adjusted again, recalled by dstr reload or powered off; they are not saved across power cycles. The configured dstr value is kept in options.txt. Overrides the Turing rate gesture; external clock takes priority.

    Checked: include dstr and recall the rate above. Unchecked: omit dstr and preserve the live density; startup then defaults to 8 Genes/sec. The module adds dstr back when it rewrites options.txt, so check the file again after a normal mount or options save.

    0.1-128 Genes/sec in 0.1 steps; default 8. Sets the startup rate and recalls the rate on settings-only reload whenever dstr is present, even if unchanged. Use the checkbox below to recall this rate or preserve the current density during settings-only reload. Requires dens 1; external clock takes priority.

    S.O.S. and feedback

    Selects the feedback mode used by SHIFT + S.O.S. and fbkp: tape overdub or classic TLA feedback. The primary S.O.S. mix is separate (sosp).

    SHIFT + S.O.S. virtual knob position, 0-100%. 0% gives no retained feedback; 100% gives maximum retention. This is the existing knob curve, not a linear gain percentage. tape selects overdub or classic TLA behavior; recalling it activates feedback control separately from the main mix.

    Primary S.O.S. virtual knob position: 0% is incoming audio, 100% is recorded Gene playback; middle positions blend them. This is separate from SHIFT + S.O.S. feedback (fbkp). Stock inop still determines what is recorded.

    Limits overlap while tape-dubbing. 0 disables the cap; 1-9 means 0.1-0.9. Higher values can increase glitches.

    Playback effects

    Selects one playback effect: sample-rate reduction or warble; they cannot run together. srrp and wrbp retain separate recalled amounts. Without an amount preset, startup uses the existing reset behavior. Warble uses less CPU than sample-rate reduction.

    SHIFT + Morph position, 0-100%: 0% bypasses SRR; increasing it strengthens sample-rate reduction. Active with fxsl 1. This is the knob curve, not a bit-depth or sample-rate percentage. Stored separately from warble; fxsl 0 bypasses both effects.

    SHIFT + Morph position, 0-100%: 0% is no warble; higher values add stronger wow/flutter. Active with fxsl 2. This is the knob curve, not a pitch percentage. Stored separately from SRR; only the selected effect runs.

    Turing patterns

    SHIFT + Organize: evolution; SHIFT + Slide: repeating scatter; SHIFT + Gene Size: Genes per step when dens is off.

    SHIFT + Organize position, 0-100%, used with sldm 1. 0% locks the repeating pattern; 100% produces fresh randomness. Intermediate values follow the existing nonlinear knob curve, not a literal probability percentage.

    Choose 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 or 16 Genes per step. Used with sldm 1; dens 1 takes priority over the Turing rate. Unchecked preserves the live Turing rate on reload.

    Number of repeating random steps, 1-16.

    Chance of choosing the root rather than a chord ratio from mcr1-mcr3. 100 disables pitch changes.

    Splice selection

    Splice number, integer 1-300; 1 is the first splice. A number above the loaded reel count selects its last splice while keeping your configured number. Unchecked keeps normal startup selection and preserves the current splice on settings-only reload.

    Input and diagnostics

    Stock gain index, 0-3. REC held + SHIFT taps changes it on the module; the mod saves it after about 3 seconds.

    Always copies L to both channels and ignores R. Auto copies L only while R is quiet.

    Writes cpulog.txt periodically. Leave off for normal performance.

    ADVANCED LIVE WORKFLOW

    Swap settings. Keep playing.

    Trusted cards only: no reel-content verification. Prepare cards with identical reel names, WAV contents and splice files; change only options.txt. Same card capacity alone is not enough. A mismatched card can cause wrong saves or overwrite audio.

    Recording, splice edits or reel changes make other copies stale. Synchronize them again before the next settings-only swap. Do not record or edit while the card is out.

    1. With the card mounted and ordinary card activity finished, stop recording and remove the card. Wait for the mounted SHIFT light to go out.
    2. Hold SHIFT alone for one second, until its LED is steady. Arming is unavailable while a card is mounted. Avoid knob movement or other buttons.
    3. Keep SHIFT held and insert the prepared matching card. Two short flashes mean success; three mean failure. Keep the card inserted through confirmation, then release SHIFT.
    4. Success keeps the live reel and card mounted for recording/saving. Omitted presets preserve live parameters; an explicit splice preset intentionally changes the selected splice. Failure leaves playback settings unchanged and the card unmounted. Remove it and mount normally to recover.
    5. To load a different reel, use normal mounting without the armed hold. That retains the usual playback interruption.
    Version by version

    mg204+c2 — Return to 1:1 and prepared presets

    Optional half-second SHIFT hold/release recalls exact forward +1x without changing short-tap splice navigation. Optional +1x at calibrated noon works across layouts, ranges and scales, with a 48-52% notch.

    All dialable values can be included in presets, with independent recall checkboxes, live reload and soft pickup. Adds splice recall with last-splice fallback, separate SRR/warble amounts, reliable effects-off behavior, clearer editor guidance, reorganized controls and updated manuals/quick cards. Rewritten options identify firmware version 204+c2.

    mg204+c — Initial release

    Extended +/-4x or forward 8x Varispeed, normal/SHIFT quantization and custom scales, independent 0.1-128 Genes/sec density with startup/live recall, switchable Morph Loop, and trusted-card settings-only reload that preserves the live reel and leaves the card mounted. Built on Briskcat’s feedback, effects, jitter/Turing, gain, mono and SD-updater mods.

    Performance & credits

    The trusted-card reload removes full-reel scanning; SD mount, options reads and file reconnection still take time. Earlier hardware testing confirmed armed insertion and ordinary options applying without an audible playback interruption. The mg204+c2 unity and preset features passed owner hardware testing and 201 automated firmware tests, including combined playback/recording comparisons. Exact latency and every performance combination have not been measured. High Morph, short Genes, dense scheduling and overdubbing can exhaust CPU headroom. Try lower density/overlap, the tape Morph cap, or warble instead of SRR.
