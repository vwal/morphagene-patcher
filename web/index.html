    THE EXTENDED VERSION

    From pitch to performance.

    A single build, with options for the way you play. All extended range, density, normal quantization and Morph Loop switches default to off.

    Pitch & timing

    Expanded Varispeed

    Enable vrng 1 for +/-4x, or combine it with vsop 2 for stop to forward 8x. Scale locking works across the expanded range.

    Pitch & timing

    Quantize your way

    Choose octaves, fifths, semitones or a custom scale. vqnt 1 keeps pitch quantized normally; SHIFT + Varispeed temporarily frees it. The default vqnt 0 retains SHIFT-to-lock.

    Pitch & timing

    Independent Gene density

    With dens 1, startup density comes from dstr, with 8 Genes/sec as the default. Hold SHIFT and turn Gene Size to adjust it from 0.1 to 128 Genes/sec, independently of pitch and Morph. Knob changes remain until adjusted again, recalled by dstr reload or powered off; they are not saved across power cycles. Set dstr to choose a startup and card-recall rate (0.1-128, in 0.1 steps). In the options editor, check “Recall density on settings-only reload” to recall dstr, or uncheck it to preserve the live rate. At 0.1, a new Gene is requested every ten seconds. The knob midpoint remains 8/sec. External clock takes priority; dense, long Genes remain limited by available voices.

    Pitch & timing

    Morph Loop, on demand

    Set mlop 1 for seamless looping at the bottom of Morph. Set mlop 0 for the original gaps. Included in mg204+c, disabled by default.

    Sound & movement

    Tape feedback & playback effects

    SHIFT + S.O.S. controls tape overdub feedback or classic TLA feedback. SHIFT + Morph adds sample-rate reduction or tape warble. The Morph cap helps keep tape overdubbing within the CPU budget.

    Sound & movement

    Jitter & Turing patterns

    SHIFT + Slide scatters Gene start positions. Enable sldm 1 for repeating random steps: SHIFT + Organize controls evolution, and SHIFT + Gene Size sets Genes per step when independent density is off.

    Live workflow

    Trusted-card settings reload

    Remove the idle card, hold SHIFT alone for one second, then insert a prepared matching card while still holding SHIFT. Settings apply without resetting the reel or playback position; the card remains mounted for recording and saving.

    Live workflow

    Small conveniences

    Input gain is remembered. Mono input can copy L onto both channels. Updates can be installed from SD after the first audio install. Includes Briskcat’s splice-overdub fix and flash prefetch.

    ADVANCED LIVE WORKFLOW

    Swap settings. Keep playing.

    Trusted cards only: no reel-content verification. Prepare cards with identical reel names, WAV contents and splice files; change only options.txt. Same card capacity alone is not enough. A mismatched card can cause wrong saves or overwrite audio.

    Recording, splice edits or reel changes make other copies stale. Synchronize them again before the next settings-only swap. Do not record or edit while the card is out.

    1. With the card mounted and ordinary card activity finished, stop recording and remove the card. Wait for the mounted SHIFT light to go out.
    2. Hold SHIFT alone for one second, until its LED is steady. Arming is unavailable while a card is mounted. Avoid knob movement or other buttons.
    3. Keep SHIFT held and insert the prepared matching card. Two short flashes mean success; three mean failure. Keep the card inserted through confirmation, then release SHIFT.
    4. Success keeps the reel and playback position and leaves the card mounted for recording/saving. Failure leaves playback settings unchanged and the card unmounted. Remove it and mount normally to recover.
    5. To load a different reel, use normal mounting without the armed hold. That retains the usual playback interruption.

    INSTALL & RECOVER

    First install by audio.
    Later updates by card.

    Follow Briskcat’s installation guide and Make Noise’s instructions supplied with the official update. Use a clean playback path with effects, EQ and system sounds disabled.

    Recovery: install the original official mg204.wav using the audio procedure. The mods do not replace the bootloader.

    1. Prepare. Remove the SD card. Connect the player to Left Signal IN, starting around 80% player volume.
    2. Enter the updater. Power on holding REC. Only the SPLICE window lights green; release REC.
    3. Play the generated update WAV. Windows flash during loading; all lights flash at completion. Keep power stable and playback uninterrupted.
    4. Calibrate as instructed by Make Noise. Power on holding SPLICE, turn Varispeed fully both ways, press SPLICE, then power-cycle.
    5. Later SD updates. Put mgmod.bin in the card root and power on. After successful installation it becomes mgmod.ok.

    Performance & credits

    The trusted-card reload removes full-reel scanning; SD mount, options reads and file reconnection still take time. Hardware testing confirmed armed insertion and changed options applying without an audible playback interruption. Exact latency and every performance combination have not been measured. High Morph, short Genes, dense scheduling and overdubbing can exhaust CPU headroom. Try lower density/overlap, the tape Morph cap, or warble instead of SRR.
